Methods compared

Prevalence

Uses presence/absence differences between 50 biological samples and 5 negative controls.

Frequency

Uses the inverse relationship between feature frequency and DNA concentration.

Combined

Combines prevalence and frequency evidence using decontam's documented combined method.

Threshold sensitivity

The decision boundary changes most sharply for prevalence at 0.6 and for frequency at 0.5-0.6.
A useful threshold should preferentially remove control reads without collapsing biological reads.

Method agreement

Low agreement is expected when frequency has little concentration contrast; combined is not simply the union of the two classifications.
